# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users
Customers of a multi-branch UK phone and laptop shop (8 branches across south-east London and Kent), most of them on a phone, often in a rush: a cracked screen, a dying battery, an old device to sell, a gadget to buy. They are not technical. Context is a train, a queue or the shop counter, one thumb, daylight. Secondary audiences are branch staff (who work repairs, orders, stock and payments at the counter, sometimes on a tablet or phone) and admins (who run catalogue, staff, branches and reports).

## Product Purpose
Virktech (trading as Smart Phones Repair) sells repairs, new, used and refurbished devices and accessories, and buys old devices. The site lets a customer book and track a repair, approve the price before any work starts, buy and collect or receive items, sell a device, and earn loyalty points, and lets staff and admins run all of that per branch. Success is a customer who books a repair or buys something in a few thumb taps and always knows what happens next.

## Positioning
One local shop for repairing, buying and selling phones and laptops, where you always see the price, the branch and the status.

## Conversion & proof
- Primary and secondary CTA: Book a repair and Shop devices carry equal weight on the home page. Secondary fallback: find the nearest branch.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Fix it, buy it, sell it, at a branch near you.
- Belief ladder: a branch is near me; I can see what it costs and what happens next; I stay in control (I approve the quote, I pay when I collect); I can track it.
- Proof on hand: 8 branches across south-east London and Kent. No ratings, years trading, warranty or free-diagnosis claims are confirmed, so none appear until verified. Behaviours that are true by design may be stated: approve the price before work starts, track every step, pay on collection, earn points.

## Brand Personality
Friendly, capable, tidy. A local bench you can trust, with the clarity of a good mobile app. Plain words, no jargon, no hype.

## Anti-references
The repair-stall flyer look (loud red and yellow, shouting banners, clutter, countdown timers). The generic startup look (purple-blue gradients, glass cards, rows of identical icon-and-text boxes). Also avoid dark neon and heavy corporate chain styling.

## Design Principles
Phone first, thumb first: the main action is always within reach, and a customer should never need to zoom or pinch.
Show the price, the branch and the next step on every screen that has one.
Expressive where customers decide (storefront), quiet where they get things done (customer app, staff and admin).
Only say what is true: no invented ratings, years or guarantees.
Earn trust through clarity: honest empty, error and out-of-stock states, and never a surprise at checkout.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Meet WCAG AA contrast, 44px minimum touch targets, visible focus, labelled form fields, 16px minimum input text, and respect reduced-motion settings. Never rely on colour alone for status.
